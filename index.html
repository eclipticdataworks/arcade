HOW TO PLAY & CHARACTER ROLES:

• Yuki (White Shiba): Guide Yuki with Arrow Keys or WASD (or swipe on touchscreen). Eat all the bone treats to clear the stage!

• Golden Acorns: Activates Zoomies Mode! The squirrels turn scared blue—chase and catch them for bonus combo points (200, 400, 800, 1600)!

• Nutty (Red Squirrel): Aggressive chaser, directly hunts Yuki.

• Chippy (Pink Squirrel): Ambusher, cuts Yuki off from 4 tiles ahead.

• Squeaky (Cyan Squirrel): Sneaky flanker, coordinates with Nutty.

• Rusty (Orange Squirrel): Shy wanderer, flees when Yuki gets close.
